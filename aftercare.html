Aftercare

Proper aftercare is important for keeping your piercing clean and supporting healthy healing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some answers to common piercing aftercare questions.

Follow the cleaning instructions provided by your professional piercer. Use an appropriate sterile saline solution and avoid touching your piercing unnecessarily.

It is best to wait until your piercing has fully healed. Ask your professional piercer before changing your jewellery.

Avoid unnecessary handling and seek advice from a reputable professional piercer. If you have signs of infection or feel unwell, contact a healthcare professional.

Healing time depends on the piercing location and your body. Your piercer can give you guidance specific to your piercing.

General Aftercare

  1. Wash your hands before touching your piercing.
  2. Keep the piercing clean.
  3. Follow the aftercare instructions provided by your piercer.
  4. Avoid unnecessary touching or twisting of the jewellery.
  5. Contact a professional if you have concerns.

Important Information

Healing times vary depending on the type and location of the piercing. Follow the specific instructions given by your professional piercer.
